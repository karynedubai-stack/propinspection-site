Support

We answer within two working days, usually sooner.

Write to karyne.declercq@icloud.com with your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem appeared.

Where is my data?

On your device only. Use the backup button in the app before you change phone.

Reading documents asks for an access code

Reading the title deed and tenancy contract needs a code we supply. Everything else in the app works without it and without a connection.

How do I share a report?

Open the report screen, create the PDF and share it from the iOS share sheet.

Is this legal advice?

No. Prop Inspection documents the condition of a property. It does not replace legal advice.
