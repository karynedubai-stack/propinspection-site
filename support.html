Common questions

Where is my data?
On your device only. Use the backup button in the app to keep a copy before changing phone.

The document reading asks for an access code.
Reading the title deed and tenancy contract needs a code supplied by us. Everything else in the app works without it and without a connection.

How do I share a report?
Open the report screen, create the PDF and share it from the iOS share sheet.

Is this legal advice?
No. PropInspection documents the condition of a property. It does not replace legal advice.

PropInspection is a documentation tool, not legal advice.
