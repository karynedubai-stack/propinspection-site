PropInspection

Move-in and move-out reports, done properly.

Photograph every room, compare entry and exit side by side, collect signatures on screen and export a clear PDF. Built for letting agents in Dubai.

Entry inspection

  • A room-by-room checklist that adapts to the property
  • Timestamped photos, notes, meter readings, keys counted
  • Tenant and agent sign on screen

Exit inspection

  • Every item shows its entry condition and photos
  • Wear, damage and missing keys flagged
  • Proposed deductions and a side-by-side PDF
