Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Prop Inspection is a mobile app that helps letting agents prepare move-in and move-out inspection reports. This page explains what happens to your data.

What stays on your device

Properties, tenant and landlord details, inspection answers, photos, signatures and the PDF reports you create are stored on your device only. We do not receive them and we cannot read them. If you delete the app or clear its data, they are removed. Use the backup export in the app to keep a copy.

What is sent when you read documents

If you choose "Read the documents and fill in", the pages of the title deed, tenancy contract or addendum that you select are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards them to Anthropic (the Claude AI service) to extract details such as the building, unit, rent and dates.

This happens only after you tick the consent box, and only for the pages you select. Our server does not store the pages or the extracted text. Anthropic processes them under its own terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). The extracted details are returned to your device. The app asks the service not to extract identity numbers.

Camera, photos and files

The camera and photo library are used only to attach photos and documents to an inspection. Access is requested by iOS and can be withdrawn in Settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, show advertising, or track you across other apps or websites. The app has no user accounts and no analytics.

Your responsibility for third-party data

Inspection reports contain information about tenants and landlords. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to record and share it, and for sharing reports only with the people concerned.

Retention and deletion

Because data stays on your device, you control it. Deleting an inspection, a property or the app removes it. Documents sent for reading are not kept by our server.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
